MSU-IIT Wildcats Receive First-Ever Institute-Level Recognition

The MSU-IIT WILDCATS proudly mark a historic milestone as they receive their very first Institute-level award; the Gawad Serbisyo 2025 , presented on August 13, 2025 by the Office of the Vice Chancellor for Student Services and the Office of Student Development Services

The Gawad Serbisyo recognizes exemplary service, dedication, and commitment to the Institute's values. This achievement reflects the WILDCATS' passion for excellence, teamwork, and their unwavering drive to contribute positively to the MSU-IIT community.

"This is not merely an accolade; it is our first step toward a wonderful future ahead," the WILDCATS expressed. "It symbolizes the beginning of a legacy, a journey where challenges will be embraced, victories will be celebrated and service will remain at the heart of everything we do."

With this reconigtion, the MSU-IIT Wildcats look forward to continuing their mission of fostering camaraderie, sportsmanship, and leadership, while setting their sights on greater achievements in the years to come.

HISTORY MADE, LEGACY SEALED

The MSU-IIT WILDCATS have once again proven their unstoppable spirit as they are crowned CHAMPIONS of Danztrack Philippines 2025 – College Division! With passion, grit, and unity, they secured a historic THREE-PEAT—their third consecutive championship title!

The Wildcats also bagged Best in Production Number and Best in Costume, showcasing their talent, creativity, and cultural pride on the grand stage.

This triumph wouldn't be possible without the unwavering support of Mindanao State University - Iligan Institute of Technology , led by Chancellor Alizedney M. Ditucalan, and the Office of the Vice Chancellor for Student Services .

Deepest gratitude to our choreographers, faculty in charge Sir Manuel Jr. Padayogdog, mentors, and the families of our performers. Special thanks to the Working Committee headed by Ken Medalla, Bagtik Apparel Ph, and our Publication Team who captured this triumph for the world to see.

Above all, we thank our supporters, alumni, fellow students, and friends who cheered, prayed, and stood with us. Your faith made every performance more powerful and every victory more meaningful.

CHOREOGRAPHERS: Justine Caylan, Nathaniel Bañares, Adrian Ray Agosto, Christian Denille Carinal, Leila Tomenio, Michael Vincent Tingson, Naina Anjali Banawa, Buds Leo Capatagan, Jhorus Seville

The MSU-IIT WILDCATS were ready to jive and jazz as they participated in The Street of Dreams: A Twin Workshop in Dance Theatre and Technical Theatre held on October 22 to 24, 2025.
